      About

      Pick any two actors and Six Degrees of Hollywood finds the shortest chain of shared movies between them, using IMDb data.

      Actors and movies form a graph: each actor is linked to every movie they appeared in. A path goes actor → movie → actor, and the number of movies on it is the degrees of separation.

      Every search runs two algorithms on the same graph and compares them:

      • BFS explores outward from the first actor, one level at a time.
      • Bidirectional BFS searches from both actors at once and stops when the two searches meet, which usually visits far fewer nodes.
